Gmail Sorter — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

Gmail Sorter is a personal tool operated by Tim Chang to organise his own Gmail mailbox. It is single-user and not offered to the public.

What it accesses

Using the Gmail API with the gmail.modify scope, the app reads email metadata and content to classify, label, archive, and move messages within the same Gmail account. The gmail.modify scope does not permit permanent deletion of mail.

How email content is used

Email subjects and body text are read to decide each message’s label and lifecycle. To classify messages, some email content is sent to Anthropic’s Claude (a third-party AI service, anthropic.com) for the sole purpose of categorisation. No email content is used for advertising, sold, or shared with any other party. Anthropic processes it under its own terms and does not train on it in the API/CLI configuration used.

Storage & retention

The app stores only derived metadata (labels, message/thread IDs, timestamps) and an undo log on the owner’s own machines. It does not retain copies of email bodies beyond transient processing. OAuth tokens are stored on the owner’s machines and can be revoked anytime at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Deletion

The app never permanently deletes mail. “Staging” only applies a label; the owner removes anything permanently.
