Privacy Policy: Gmail Sorter (personal use)

Last updated: 2 October 2026

Gmail Sorter ("the app") is a personal tool operated by Tim Chang to organise his own Gmail mailbox. It has one user, the account owner, and only processes that account's data.

What the app accesses

Through the Gmail API (scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.modify) the app reads email content and metadata, and applies labels, archives and moves email within the same account. This scope cannot permanently delete mail, and the app never does.

How data is used

  • Email content is used only to decide each email's label and whether to keep, archive or stage it for the owner's review.
  • To classify email, the app sends the relevant email text to Anthropic's Claude AI service, using the owner's own Claude account. No other third party receives email content. Nothing is sold, shared for advertising, or used for analytics.

Storage and retention

  • A working copy of email content and the app's decisions are stored only on the owner's own computer, to run the app and keep an undo log.
  • OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's own computers and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.

Sharing

None, beyond the AI classification described above.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Gmail Sorter's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
